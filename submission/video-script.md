# RAY Shop: 90-second video script

Record at 1440x900 or wider. Open `https://ray-shop.vercel.app/?pace=fast#overview` (the fast pace runs the whole demo in about 50 seconds).

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:12 | Overview hero, then scroll to Meet Meera | "Meera bakes from home in Bengaluru. Orders come as Instagram DMs, payment is a UPI screenshot, and reminders are typed one chat at a time. Free UPI gives her money in the bank and nothing else, so she never needs Razorpay." |
| 0:12 to 0:22 | Scroll back up to the hero | "RAY Shop puts her shop inside WhatsApp, run by Razorpay's RAY. Claude reads the mess, Meera's rules do the money, and she approves anything a customer sees." |
| 0:22 | Click **Try RAY Shop now** | (one click; the demo plays itself) |
| 0:23 to 0:32 | Menu photo, Publish shop | "She sends a photo of her menu. RAY reads it, she taps Publish, and her shop page is live." |
| 0:32 to 0:47 | 3 months later, Riya's order, Send payment link, checkout paid | "A Hinglish DM becomes a priced order: rules, not AI, work out Rs 1,180. One tap sends a Razorpay link, and Riya pays." |
| 0:47 to 0:58 | Fake screenshot, BLOCKED in the trace | "Arjun sends a 'paid' screenshot. Razorpay has no such payment, so RAY says don't bake yet." |
| 0:58 to 1:10 | Reminders, Diwali offer | "It reminds only the customers who owe, and sends the Diwali offer only to the 27 who said yes." |
| 1:10 to 1:22 | Click the Growth tab | "Then it shows what sells, what to drop, and when she is ready for Payment Pages, Checkout and a loan. She starts on Razorpay on day one, and grows with it." |
| 1:22 to 1:28 | Back to the Overview | "RAY Shop. Try it at ray-shop.vercel.app." |

About 190 words spoken. If you run long, cut the Growth line short.
